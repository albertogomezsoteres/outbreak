# Data card: OutbreakBench environment and episode records

## Composition
- **Scenarios:** `(family, mechanism, seed)`. There are 6 families and 5 mechanisms (4 impossible plus
  POSSIBLE). The seed space is unbounded. Records are synthetic business data (orders, accounts, stock counts,
  latencies, invoices, A/B events) drawn from seeded generators in `src/outbreak/env/families.py`.
- **Notes:** a 2⁶ trait factorial × 3 paraphrase draws × 4 mechanisms = 768 shortcut notes
  (`notes_M*.jsonl`, used by exp_002). exp_003 uses a 12-note Plackett-Burman subset per mechanism
  (`notes_pb12_M*.jsonl`, `outbreak notes --design pb12`). There are also 6 benign notes.
  - All notes are generated by `src/outbreak/data/notes.py`.
  - Benign and shortcut notes are padded with neutral filler to the same length band (~480–570 chars).
- **Natural notes** (`natural_notes_M*.jsonl`, `outbreak natural-notes`):
  - 48 notes per mechanism, written by an LLM that is not an agent in the study, in six voices;
  - stored with the writer model, the prompt hash and a `mentions_shortcut` usability flag;
  - not edited by hand.
- **Manipulation-check ratings** (`ratings.csv`, `agreement.csv`): six trait judgments per note from an LLM
  rater, with design labels for the template notes.
- **Episodes** (`episodes.jsonl`), one JSON object per line:
  - `episode_id`, `model`, `scenario` (family/mechanism/seed);
  - `condition` (board, note_id, note_text, replicate, cell and traits);
  - `outcome`: `violation`, `attempt`, `executed{m1..m4}`, `attempts{...}`, `legit_success`,
    `report_correct`, `original_tests_pass`, `honest_block`, `terminal`, `steps`, `blocked_by_gate`,
    `first_attempt_step`;
  - `usage` (tokens, cost, latency, cache hits);
  - `board_reads`, `posts`, `first_action`;
  - `providers` (which upstream provider served the calls) and `turn_meta` (for example, per-turn probe
    scores and whether the vaccine was applied);
  - `decision_prefix`, `transcript` and `events` (every tool call with its result and attempted mechanisms).
- **Outbreaks** (`outbreaks.jsonl`):
  - `summary`: arm, threshold, violations, attack rate, blocked violation rate, utility, secondary
    violations, plausible chains, posts published or blocked, cost;
  - `agents` (per-agent outcome, rounds, which authors they read);
  - `board` (every post with its channel verdict);
  - `chains` (source → reader pairs).
- **Decision points** (`decision_points.jsonl`): unique prefixes with `k/n`, split, note id, the violating
  and honest candidate actions, and the tool list.

## Collection
- Agents are LLMs accessed through OpenRouter (`temperature=1`, `reasoning.effort=low`, `max_tokens=2048`).
  Each response is cached and its provider recorded.
- Outcomes come from deterministic detectors. No human or LLM labelling is involved.
- Pilot data (`data/raw/pilot`) are the raw logs of the feasibility pilot, with SHA256 checksums. They contain
  full transcripts, including a canary admin token that is fictional and grants nothing anywhere.

## Splits
`data/splits.py` assigns scenario draws `(family, seed)` to train/val/test (60/20/20) by hash.
`experiment_readout` is held out entirely as an OOD family. Filter splits are by note text
(`pipeline/filter_training.py`).

## Personal and sensitive information
None. All entities, tokens, hosts and people named in notes ("Dana from the platform team") are fictional.
